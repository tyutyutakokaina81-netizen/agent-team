【English】Melon — the luxury of scooping with a spoon.

You cut a well-chilled melon in half on the board, and as the knife slides in, that sweet, fine scent rises up. You scoop out the seeds in the center with a spoon, then plunge the spoon straight into the hollow and eat the flesh right out of it — no dish needed; the half-melon is itself the dish. A melon is, somehow, a bit of a special presence among fruits. Watermelon or peach you bite into casually; but a melon has a certain "feast" dignity — that round, round shape, the netted pattern, and that deep, refined sweetness.

The tricky thing about a melon is its ripeness. You don't eat it right after buying; left a while to ripen, the sweetness increases, they say — but leave it too long and it over-ripens and the taste goes vague, and judging that just-right moment is fairly hard. Press the bottom gently, and if it's a little soft, it's about time, they say; when the sweet scent grows strong, it's near. In our house we go by that scent and feel, the family conferring, "about now?" — and that time of waiting for ripeness is one of a melon's pleasures. Eating it, the sweetest part is the center, the area around the seeds most ripe and sweet, so you're tempted to spoon straight into the middle. But you hold back and eat from the outer part near the rind, saving the best for last — a way of eating I've kept since childhood, from the lighter part near the rind gradually toward the sweetest center, savoring that gradation of sweetness and taking the greatest reward at the end.

A melon isn't something we eat every day — it's when a guest comes, or some celebration, or a gift received; so when a melon appears on the table, that alone somehow makes it a little bit of a special day. The family shares the half-melon, scooping with spoons, saying "sweet, isn't it" "yeah, sweet." Not a flashy feast, but this round, round fruit has the power to make everyone a little happy — a small luxury of summer. (Variety, ripeness and judging the ripening vary by view and person; this is our one example. Mind chilling, storage and food safety.)
