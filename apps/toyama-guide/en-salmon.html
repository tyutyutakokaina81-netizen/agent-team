Caught at Sea on Its Way to a River

The fish is born in a river and caught before it can get back to one.

Salmon are known for swimming upstream to spawn. What reaches the table is taken at sea.

Taken before the return

Salmon netted offshore is what fills the shop — as fillets, sometimes whole.

You cannot simply take them from a river. I am told a permit is required, and I have never seen anyone fishing for salmon in one.

So a line is drawn one step before the part everyone knows about: the fish that returns to a river, and the fillet you buy, are separated by that line.

I have never seen the run

They do return to rivers in this part of the country. But the timing and the place shift from year to year, and it is not the kind of thing you can go and watch on demand.

Some years there are many and some years few. I could not give you numbers.

I know it as information and have never stood in front of it. With salmon, I have stayed in that state.

The salted version came first

Salted salmon, on the other hand, I have eaten since childhood.

Grilled, with a small argument over whether to eat the skin. Shredded into rice on days when picking out the bones feels like too much. Two fillets on a plate is not a special occasion.

It is the same fish, and yet the two things have never joined up in my head.

In Toyama

The sea is close, so in autumn a whole one sometimes lies on the shop counter.

Seeing it, I think for a second that this fish was partway to some river. Then I buy the fillets and go home.

Fact-check note
  • ✅ Salmon return to the river where they were born in order to spawn.
  • ✅ Most salmon sold as food is caught at sea.
  • ⚠️ Taking salmon from rivers requires a permit, and the rules differ by prefecture and by river. The text presents this as something heard and gives no procedure.
  • ⚠️ No timing, location or run-size figures are given — the variation between years is large and the writer has not checked them.
  • ⚠️ The writer states plainly that he has never seen the run.
  • ⚠️ The way salted salmon is eaten here is this household's habit, not a rule.
  • ⚠️ No river, port, shop or personal name appears (prefecture level only).
