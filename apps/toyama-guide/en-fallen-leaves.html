Swept Yesterday, the Same Amount Again Today

Sweeping leaves is the one autumn job with no end point.

I clear the front of the house and the gutter along the road, bag it up — and the next morning there is the same amount again.

It does not go down

The tree keeps dropping until it is finished, so clearing part way through does not change the total.

I know this and sweep anyway. The gutter blocks; wet leaves are slippery. There are practical reasons, so it is not pointless work. What it lacks is any sense of completion.

Every year I think the same thing: if they would all come down at once, it would take one afternoon.

Whose leaves they are is unclear

They are not only from our tree.

Wind moves them, so what lies in front of the house may belong to no particular tree nearby. The neighbours are in the same position, each clearing their own frontage — everyone tidying leaves that belong to nobody.

I do not remember anyone discussing where one range ends and the next begins. And yet each year everyone stops at roughly the same place.

Where the bags wait

The bags sit beside the house until collection day.

Rain makes them heavy. After one gave out at the bottom while being lifted, I stopped moving them on wet days.

In Toyama

Finishing this means the snow preparations are next.

November brings heavier outdoor work, so the leaf season counts as the easy end of it — the entrance to a run of jobs that get harder as winter comes.

And for all the talk of no end point, the snow ends it. The season settles the matter before the leaves do.

Fact-check note
  • ✅ Deciduous trees shed over a period rather than all at once.
  • ⚠️ "The same amount again the next day" is how it feels, not a measurement.
  • ⚠️ How leaves are sorted and collected differs by municipality. No categories or collection days are given.
  • ⚠️ "Neighbours each clear their own frontage" is what the writer sees; it is not a local rule, and whether any agreement exists was not checked.
  • ⚠️ "November brings the snow preparations" is the writer's sense of the year, not a statistic. It shifts between years.
  • ⚠️ The bag giving way happened at this house; it is not a comment on any product.
  • ⚠️ No district or personal name appears, and nothing identifies a neighbouring house (prefecture level only).
