夏になると、靴を、はかなくなる。玄関に、ぽんと、置いてある、サンダルを、つっかけて、そのまま、外へ出る。鼻緒に、親指と、人差し指のあいだを、ひっかけて。ぺたん、ぺたん、と、歩きだす。靴下も、いらない。ひもを、結ぶ手間も、ない。この、気軽さが、たまらない。夏は、もう、ほとんど、これ一足で、過ごしている。

サンダルの、いいところは、なんといっても、その、開放感だ。足が、外の空気に、触れている。むれない。暑い夏に、靴と靴下で、足を、蒸らすのは、考えただけで、うんざりする。その点、サンダルなら、足が、すーすーして、気持ちがいい。夏の、足もとの、自由。あれを、知ってしまうと、もう、戻れない。

【English】Sandals — one pair does the summer.

Come summer, you stop wearing shoes. You slip on the sandals sitting by the door and step straight out, the thong hooked between your big toe and the next, and set off petan, petan. No socks needed, no laces to tie — that ease is irresistible, and by summer you spend almost the whole season in this one pair. The best of sandals is, above all, the openness: your feet touch the outside air, no stuffiness. In a hot summer, steaming your feet in shoes and socks — the mere thought is dispiriting; with sandals the feet go airy and it feels good. That freedom of summer feet — once you know it, there's no going back.

Walking in sandals makes a distinctive sound: petan, petan, or patan, patan, the heel slapping the sandal's sole, that sound trailing you at each step; on a quiet road, your footsteps alone ring oddly loud. This sound, somehow, suits summer's slack feeling well — the very opposite of a brisk leather shoe's sound; making that goofy sound, you amble slackly down a hot road, in no mood to hurry, the sandal's sound a kind of BGM for a summer's easygoing mood. Sandals have a weakness, though: they slip off easily. Try to hurry into a trot and the heel doesn't keep up, one flapping half-off, and you hurriedly grip the thong with your toes — you can't sprint in sandals. And off guard, the thong chafes the base of your toes sore — thong-rub; the newer the sandal, the more prone to it, and at summer's start, breaking out sandals after a while, you usually get baptized by that chafe once. Still, unchastened, you wear them again — summer can't be had without sandals.

At summer's end, sandals leave a parting gift: the tan line on the top of your foot, white and tanned divided cleanly in the shape of the thong or straps; seeing that mark, you think, "I walked around in sandals a lot again this year." No grand footwear — one cheap pair of beach sandals gets you through summer fine; but with this easy, open thing on your feet, you somehow endure the summer heat. Slipping on the sandals by the door, out into the hot day again today — a dependable summer companion. (Comfort varies by person; this is our one example. Sandals can be unsuited to long walks or rough, hazardous ground — take care with slipping off, thong-rub, tripping and injury.)
