【English】Sweat — however much you wipe.

Walk outside just a little and the sweat already gushes: it beads on your forehead, then runs in a thin line down your temple and drops; your back, too, the shirt clinging damply. Summer means, above all, sweat — however much you sweat, more comes after, and after that; the summer body is like a sweat factory. Sweating is, honestly, a nuisance — sticky, unpleasant; change your clothes and you're soon wet with sweat again, makeup runs, hair clings, that always-damp feeling of summer is fairly hard to bear. But sweating is, it seems, also proof that the body is properly fighting the heat.

Come summer, a towel becomes indispensable — one draped around the neck, and the moment you sweat you wipe it: forehead, neck, arms. Whether you have that towel or not makes all the difference to summer comfort; on a summer outing, a towel is a must-carry. The towel you wipe with turns damp after a while, and hung in the sun it dries crisp at once — absorbing sweat, drying, absorbing again, drying again; a summer towel earns its keep, and however many you wash, there are never quite enough to soak up the sweat.

A shower after a good sweat is something special: rinsing off the sticky body, that fresh feeling the instant you wash the sweat away — a reward you can taste only because you sweated plenty; without sweating, you'd never know the gratitude of that shower. Then you change into a dry, clean shirt, and the sweat-wet body goes smooth and dry, and you feel revived. This cycle of sweating, rinsing, changing — a bother, but you could almost say you spend the summer for the sake of that fresh moment. Sweating is a nuisance, no mistake; yet a summer without sweat feels somehow flavorless too. Drenched, moving around outside, worn to nothing — that's part of summer too; that feeling of sweating and playing about as a kid was a kind of symbol of summer's health. Of course sweating too much is hard on the body, so I take plenty of water and salt and don't overdo it; still, a little sweat is proof of living summer to the full, and today too, sweating, I somehow get through this hot summer. (How you sweat and feel it varies by person; this is our one example. In a summer of heavy sweating, mind hydration, salt and rest, and guard against heatstroke; if you feel unwell, don't push yourself and consult a medical professional as needed.)
