【English】A peach — bite in, and it runs down to your wrist.

A well-ripened peach is almost scary just to hold: press a finger even a little and it seems it might dent right there, that soft. Peel the fuzzed skin gently and the juicy flesh shows underneath; one bite, and the juice runs down your chin toward your wrist in a thin trail. When I eat a peach I'm usually standing at the sink — something that drips that much is hard to eat neatly with plate and fork, so I hold it in my hand, bite in, and let the drips fall into the sink. Not elegant, but for a peach alone I think that's the best way to eat it.

There are, roughly, two camps: those who like it melting-soft and ripe, and those who like it a touch firm with a crisp bite. Neither is right; the preference just splits people cleanly. I'm in the soft camp — that helpless give when your finger presses in a little is, to me, the eating moment; and yet a firm peach's crunch is hard to give up too, so in the end I like both, and honestly, any peach will do. Peaches are delicate above all: leave one a few days by mistake and it turns too soft, part of it spoiling in no time; chill it too hard, they say, and the taste goes vague, so cool it only shortly before eating. That difficulty of handling makes a peach a little special — eat it soon or it's ruined — so with one in the house you feel oddly hurried, and that being-hurried is its own mark of the short peach season.

Where is a peach sweetest? Just under the skin, I think, so I don't peel it thick, unwilling to lose that sweetest edge. The peeled skin, sucked, is still sweet; as a kid I'd suck the skins, unable to waste them, and I still do it in secret — suck it all, right down to the awkward parts around the pit, if no one's watching. A peach makes me greedy. Soft, sweet, quick to spoil, and it leaves your hands sticky — a high-maintenance fruit. But for the sweetness that fills your mouth at the bite, and that running juice, a little stickiness is cheap. (Firmness, ripeness and where it's sweetest vary by taste and view; this is our one example. Peaches spoil easily, so mind storage and ripeness, chilling and food safety.)
