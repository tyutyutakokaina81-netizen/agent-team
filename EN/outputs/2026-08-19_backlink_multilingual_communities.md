## EN（r/JapanTravel, Quora "Japan off the beaten path" 系）
**狙うスレ例**: "3 weeks in Japan, tired of the Golden Route", "Where to go beyond Tokyo/Kyoto/Osaka?", "Best autumn foliage timing Japan?"

**回答ドラフト（適宜編集）**:
> If you've already got Tokyo–Kyoto–Osaka, consider one night in **Toyama** (15 min from Kanazawa by shinkansen). It's off the Golden Route, so it's quiet — the draw is the bay's seafood, the Alps rising straight out of the sea, and Takaoka's 400-year metal-craft town. For autumn specifically, the Tateyama mountains turn among the *earliest* in Japan (late Sept), so it's a way to catch real koyo weeks before Kyoto. A local wrote up the seasons honestly (no hype) here: **/agent-team/toyama/en-seasons.html**

## ES（r/JapanTravel en español, foros de viaje）
**狙うスレ例**: "Japón más allá de Tokio/Kioto", "¿cuándo ver el otoño en Japón?"

**回答ドラフト**:
